## Decisions and assumptions
All of these are logged one per line in DECISIONS.md.
- **Path reconstruction.** Q-PATH uses `distance(from_=OurSeeds)` plus `distance(from_=TopThreeCEOs)` on the undirected Knows graph, with fixed-length Knows joins in rules. `from_` and `to` cannot be combined in 1.27.1.
- **Exact betweenness.** Use `betweenness_centrality(num_pivots >= num_nodes())`. The default is sampled with 100 pivots, so it is approximate and non-deterministic.
- **Louvain.** Visual only; no golden assertion.
- **Scoring.** Fit max 30 + Readiness capped at 50 + Access (CRM 10 in Act 1; warm path 10 in the Finale). Tier A >= 70. Ties break on readiness desc, then fit desc, then name asc. CONF and T_5 tie at 75.
- **Top-three order.** T_BANKER (90, 3 hops), T_WARM (85, 2 hops), T_COLD (80, cold). This deliberately swaps 7.3's target 1/target 2 numbering so the Finale flips the #1 spot.
- **T_COLD's last recap** is a 2023 dividend recap arranged by Harbor Lane. That satisfies both exit-window readiness (held since 2020) and the 5-year Q-FLOW window.
- **R02 corrections adopted:**
  - exit window = held 5+ years AND fund 8+ years past vintage;
  - "majority" add-ons with no number;
  - DealCloud also has warm paths;
  - deal_role / seller_type / structure.
- **Hero CRM touches are org-level**, so they create no Knows edges that would shorten planted paths.
