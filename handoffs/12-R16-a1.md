## Decisions and assumptions

Nine R16 lines are in DECISIONS.md. The key ones:
- Every value is a `<span data-k data-f>` and is re-checked after the build.
- A visible digit outside an injected value fails the check. The only exempt containers are `[data-src]` (prompts, the domain brief, story cards) and `.lit` (act numbers and minutes).
- The template audit forbids digits in visible text.
- Act order is `meta.act_order`, else `meta.act3`, else the v2 order.
- Prompts come from QUESTIONS.md v2 until prompts.md drops "DRAFT v1".
- Story content appears only if all of its numbers equal exports.
