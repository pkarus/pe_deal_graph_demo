## Decisions and assumptions

The DECISIONS.md R09 lines cover these. The main ones:
- **The Finale is opt-in.** It writes only finale_* names, so Act-1 answers are unchanged when it is on.
- **BuyingFund.** It is computed from Fund.sponsor and not from OurFund. Aggregating over its own supertype is recursion through an aggregate.
- **Evidence selection.** Each score component's evidence comes from ScoreCandidate rows, ranked per company and code: newest signal, earliest maturity, longest founder tenure, earliest prior owner.
- **Ordered text lists.** They are built by a recursive prefix rule. The end of the list is detected through a stored item relation. Two things do not work: `string_join`, and identity accessors inside `not_`.
- **String group keys.** A String group key must be grounded by a real row before an aggregate can create entities. The probe confirmed this: without it, zero entities are created.
- **why_not (b).** It is long when it stands alone and short after template (a). The short form exactly matches golden D_SHORT.
- **Exposure labels.** PortfolioExposure.kind is "CONFLICT - cross-fund review". The Finale flag is "Conflict - cross-fund review".
