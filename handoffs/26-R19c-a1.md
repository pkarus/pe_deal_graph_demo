## Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. Founder and CEO tenures imply executives aged 75 to 85, and some are visible in Tier B evidence.** All of these are noise-layer rows.

Script output, sections 3f and 7:
- `founder CEO total tenure: p50=29.13 p75=41.01 p95=49.72 max=53.05`
- `current founder CEOs with 35+ years in seat: 129` (all noise)
- `67 people with senior careers over 45 years`; the earliest senior start is 1966-04-12
- `non-founder CEO closed median 14.5 (signal 5.7)`
- `non-founder CEOs who start within a year of founding: 14 (closed median 26.9 yrs)`

The signal layer is fine: founder p95 is 28.2 years.

The oracle shows these tenures in Tier B (50-69) R_SUCCESSION evidence. Claude Code will print them if asked for Tier B or near misses:
- "founder CEO Jasper Calloway since 1976-04-07 = 50.48 years" (Eldermont Chemicals)
- "Soren Rasmussen since 1977-04-26 = 49.43 years" (Hobley Geotechnical Labs)
- "Nora Blackwood since 1983 = 42.88 years" (Quillmarsh Adjusting Services)

Tenure is the guardrail's stand-in for age. At this tail it becomes an age signal and is not credible.

Fix, in the noise section of `data/generate.py` only:
- A noise founder stays CEO only if founded_year is 1994 or later.
- Otherwise the founder's tenure ends at founded + U(12, 30) years, and non-founder successor CEOs follow. Draw successor tenures lognormal, median 6y, p5 3y, p95 12y, chained to the present with no overlap. Reuse the R12 filler-CEO pattern: new persons, no other role, no shared employer with any seed.
- Cap any single career at 40 years.

Golden safety:
- Signal roles are untouched.
- On noise companies R_SUCCESSION can only be lost, so no non-cast score rises.
- The successors are Knows-graph leaves, so warm paths are unchanged.

Re-run `make data-noise` (SELF-CHECK and NOISE-CHECK) and the oracle-vs-golden script. Expect the noise Tier B count to change, which is not golden.
