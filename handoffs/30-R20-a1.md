## Decisions and assumptions
- **M2, no OWNERSHIP rows for add-on targets.** R_EXIT_WINDOW, R_CFO_FROM_PE and NorthwindHolding read any ownership row whatever its stake_type, so such rows could raise scores or create Northwind holdings. The acquired status is carried instead by:
  - the TRANSACTION add-on row
  - the division-president title
  - no pipeline row, signals or CRM after the close
- **M2, CEO role type.** role_type stays CEO, so R08's one-current-CEO invariant needs no waiver. The audit line "405 of 405 still have a current CEO row" therefore stays at 405 by design.
- **M1, partial CEO history.** At most the current CEO and one predecessor are recorded, so the history is deliberately incomplete. Full chains back to founding would cost about 1,500 rows.
- **Noise stream shifted.** The noise rows downstream of `company_people` changed, though none of them is golden. The new Fund III cap of 45 was needed to keep the G2 "extra flagged are Tier C" check green.
